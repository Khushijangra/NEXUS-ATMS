# Dataset License Report

## Phase B — Licensing Audit

### 1. AI City Challenge
* **License type**: Academic / Data Sharing Agreement.
* **Academic usage permissions**: Allowed strictly for non-commercial research and challenge participation.
* **Commercial restrictions**: Strictly prohibited.
* **Citation requirements**: Requires citing the official AI City Challenge workshop papers (e.g., CVPR workshops).

### 2. UA-DETRAC
* **License type**: Non-commercial Research License.
* **Academic usage permissions**: Allowed for academic research.
* **Commercial restrictions**: Strictly prohibited without explicit prior consent.
* **Citation requirements**: Requires citing the original UA-DETRAC benchmark paper (Wen et al.).

### 3. DoTA (Dashcam Obstacle/Anomaly)
* **License type**: Academic Non-commercial.
* **Academic usage permissions**: Allowed for evaluating anomaly detection and action recognition.
* **Commercial restrictions**: Strictly prohibited.
* **Citation requirements**: Requires citing the DoTA paper (Yao et al.).

### 4. inD (Intersection Drone Dataset)
* **License type**: Academic License Agreement (RWTH Aachen).
* **Academic usage permissions**: Allowed only after submitting a formal request form signed by a permanent academic supervisor or researcher.
* **Commercial restrictions**: Strictly prohibited. Commercial licenses must be negotiated separately.
* **Citation requirements**: Requires citing the inD dataset paper (Bock et al., IV 2020).

**Conclusion**: All datasets are legally usable for scientific research, provided the principal investigator signs the respective data access agreements. The strict academic restrictions mean we cannot bypass the official download gates.
